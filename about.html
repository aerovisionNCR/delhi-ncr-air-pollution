ABOUT THE PROJECT

AERO VISION

Air Pollution–Weather Coupled Forecasting System for Delhi NCR.

THE PROBLEM

Why This System?

Air pollution in urban regions is strongly influenced by weather conditions. Temperature, humidity, wind, atmospheric stability and boundary-layer conditions can affect the concentration and movement of pollutants.

Traditional air-quality forecasting approaches may consider pollution and meteorological information separately. A coupled approach combines these factors to improve understanding of pollution behaviour.

OUR APPROACH

Integrated Forecasting

01

IoT Monitoring

Environmental sensors collect air-quality and weather-related measurements.

02

Weather Coupling

Meteorological parameters are combined with pollutant measurements.

03

AI Prediction

Machine-learning and time-series models analyse the collected data.

04

Early Forecast

The system is designed to provide pollution forecasts for up to 72 hours.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Our Goal

The objective of AERO VISION is to develop an integrated air-pollution and weather forecasting platform that combines IoT observations, meteorological information and artificial intelligence.

The platform is designed to help visualize current pollution conditions and generate future AQI forecasts, supporting better awareness of air-quality conditions across Delhi NCR.
