About AERO VISION

AERO VISION is an air pollution and weather monitoring platform designed to study pollution conditions across Delhi NCR and provide useful forecasting information.

🌍 Our Project

AERO VISION combines air pollution data, weather conditions and intelligent forecasting techniques into a single web-based dashboard.


The platform is designed to help users understand current pollution conditions and observe how weather factors can influence air quality.

🎯 Our Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a simple and informative system for monitoring and forecasting air pollution in Delhi NCR.


The system presents pollution measurements, weather information, forecasts, locations and sensor information in an easy-to-understand format.
